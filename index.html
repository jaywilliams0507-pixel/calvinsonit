How it works

Here's how a job works.

Most tech help bills by the hour. We agree on the finished result and the price before we start.

  1. Tell us what's broken

    Pick a service, or describe the problem to Calvin in a few sentences.

  2. Get the price

    Listed services have a set price. Custom work gets a written quote first.

  3. We do the work

    We'll ask a few questions, then handle the rest. Most jobs need no meetings.

  4. You get it, checked

    The finished work lands in your inbox.
