Who we are

This notice covers the CalvinsonIT website and information provided for our business services.

Information you provide

When you contact us or order a service, you may provide your name, email address, business details, website, order information and files needed for the work.

We will use this information to answer your request, complete your order, provide support and keep required business records. Please send only what the job needs. Do not send passwords, full card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information.

The website and payments

Cloudflare hosts this protected staging preview and provides access controls and security logs. The live site remains on GitHub Pages pending an approved cutover. Supabase provides managed sign-in, order records and private file storage. Google handles Google sign-in when you choose it. The website loads Google Fonts, so Google receives the network information needed to deliver those fonts.

The website includes no analytics or advertising tracking code.

Stripe processes payments; this preview uses test mode only. Resend is the planned provider for transactional order updates and internal lead/order alerts, subject to sender configuration. Supabase sends sign-in links and Stripe sends its receipts. Transactional delivery providers receive the address and content needed for those messages. In Stripe-hosted checkout, your full card number is entered with Stripe, not on this website.

Use of AI and your choices

When a job uses AI services to help prepare work, we will share only the information needed for that job.

We will not sell your personal information. We send only transactional email, such as sign-in links, receipts and order updates. We do not send marketing email or create mailing lists. These services are for business customers aged 18 or older.

How long information is kept

The client-file retention policy is 90 days after delivery. Staging test files are reviewed and removed by an authorized operator; automatic retention is not yet enabled. Use synthetic test files in this preview. Payment and tax records will be kept as long as the law requires.

Privacy requests

Email hello@calvinsonit.org to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We will answer within 30 days, or sooner if required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations. Do not send identity documents with your initial request.
