Who we are

CalvinsonIT is the legal business name. We provide fixed-price technology services for businesses across the United States. This protected preview accepts test payments only and does not create a real paid order.

Orders, payment and delivery

Prices are in US dollars, paid in full upfront. An order is accepted when payment clears through Stripe. The audit delivery clock starts after both confirmed payment and complete intake. The five-business-day calculation uses Monday through Friday in America/New_York, excluding the start date; staging applies no holiday exclusions. Catalog delivery timing and final holiday policy require confirmation before production sales.

Payments are processed by Stripe. In Stripe-hosted checkout, your full card number is entered with Stripe, not on this website.

Please provide accurate information and only files you have permission to share. Changes beyond the purchased service's scope are not included. Choose only the listed package that fits your job.

Account access

Sign-in is provided by Supabase email links or Google. Staging access is limited to admitted testers. Transactional emails may include sign-in links, receipts, order updates and service alerts. We do not send marketing email.

Do not send passwords. Where a job needs account access, use delegated access, such as a store collaborator or profile manager role. You remain the account owner and can remove access at any time. Removing access may prevent us from completing work that needs it.

Ownership and samples

Once paid, you own the report and cleaned files delivered for your order. Our tools and scripts remain ours and are not handed over. We will use your name or work as a sample only with your written permission.
